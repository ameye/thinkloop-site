Dr Ameye is an ENT surgeon and data scientist with more than two decades in medicine and a focused second career in machine learning. He was a Reader (Associate Professor) in Otorhinolaryngology at Obafemi Awolowo University and its teaching hospital, and has published 26 peer-reviewed articles and two theses spanning clinical medicine and AI.

His research sits at the intersection that matters: audiology and hearing health, clinical fuzzy systems, and the WHO hearing-loss agenda — work that has produced a validated Fuzzy Audiometric Index and a research pipeline built on honest, reproducible validation. That is exactly the discipline he now brings to clients.

Based between the GCC and West Africa, with engagements across three continents, he leads ThinkLoop's projects personally — from the first data-readiness conversation to the deployed, monitored system.
