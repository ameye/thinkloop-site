Evaluation of the Dizzy Patient

A graphical bedside manual for separating the dangerous minority of dizzy patients from the benign majority, in ten minutes, without a neuro-otologist.

What it is for
Sorting dizziness by timing rather than by the words a patient uses, clearing the red flags before any test is chosen, and finding the stroke that presents as vertigo.
Who it is for
General physicians in primary care and emergency departments, internal medicine, and junior residents.

Nothing is listed here before it is ready. There are no placeholders and no tiles marked coming soon: if a tool appears on this page, it is written, checked and published, and the file behind it works.

Why these are free

Published, not sold

These are the tools we would want in our own pockets. Putting them in the public domain costs us nothing and saves a colleague an evening.

Free, and without a form

No registration, no email capture, no paywall. A clinician at two in the morning should not have to request a download link.

Named and versioned

Every tool carries its author, its version and, where it makes clinical claims, the evidence those claims rest on.

Decision support, not protocol

These aid a qualified clinician's judgement. Where a local pathway disagrees with one of them, the local pathway wins.

Using one in teaching or in a department folder? Credit the author, keep the file unaltered, and check the version against the one published here.
